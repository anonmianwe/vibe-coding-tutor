# Demo video script (max 3:00)

For: the team member who records the demo video (submission item 3).
Upload as **unlisted YouTube** or a **Google Drive link that anyone with the link can view**.
App: https://anonmianwe.github.io/vibe-coding-tutor/

Narration is in Thai (read it aloud, or record it over the video). The times are a guide: the whole video must stay **under 3:00**.

## Before recording (about 15 minutes of prep)

- **Two browsers on the computer:**
  - **Browser A, the "student":** a fresh browser profile or incognito window, for the start screen.
  - **A second browser profile, the "prepared student":** before recording, play in it up to **chapter 4, level 1**.
    - Get **3 ⭐ on all of chapter 1**. That unlocks typing mode, step mode (5 ⭐), the green and orange Bit colors, and the chapter 1 challenge.
    - Chapters 2–3 only need to be solved. If you get stuck, the 💡 solution button appears after 2 failed runs.
    - This takes about 10 minutes, and it's needed for the 1:10–1:55 shots.
  - **Browser B, the "teacher":** make a 1–2 level pack ahead of time (home → 🏫 สำหรับคุณครู). Test each level, so the "🔗 สร้างลิงก์และ QR" (make link and QR) button is ready.
- **A phone** for the live classroom, on working internet. Test once that it joins before you record.
- **Screen recorder:** OBS, Xbox Game Bar (Win+G), or QuickTime. Record at 1080p if you can.
- **Sound:** turn the computer sound on, so the 🔊 voice is recorded. Use a quiet room for the narration.
- **Zoom** the browser to 125–150%, so text is readable in the video.
- **Phone shots:** film the phone with another phone, or mirror it to the computer (scrcpy for Android, QuickTime for iPhone).

## If something goes wrong while recording

- **The live room doesn't connect** (network): skip the phone part, and show the pack link instead. Say "ถ้าเครือข่ายบล็อก นักเรียนยังเล่นชุดด่านเดียวกันจากลิงก์ได้" (if the network blocks it, students can still play the same pack from the link).
- **No sound on 🔊:** check the computer volume. The voice files are built in, so the app doesn't need a Thai voice installed.
- **Running long:** cut the 0:55–1:10 accessibility part to 8 seconds, or skip drawing in the editor.

## Must not say

- Not "AI inside the app" (there is no AI in the app; AI was used to build it).
- Not "a real Python/C compiler" (it's a teaching subset with real syntax).
